1. Why did you choose to pursue an MBA?
During my undergraduate studies, I ranked in the top ten percentile of my cohort and participated in a specialized initiative known as Project School. Tasked with developing a software application based on a specific problem statement, I initially assumed our evaluation would center entirely on technical execution. However, the assessment panel heavily emphasized go to market strategies, customer acquisition, and project management principles. This pivotal experience revealed that programming expertise alone is insufficient for commercial product success, inspiring my decision to acquire formal management skills at a premier institution like an IIM.
2. What are your short term and long term career goals?
In the short term, my primary objective is to establish a robust academic foundation while actively engaging in case competitions to rapidly refine my business problem solving capabilities. Looking further ahead, my long term ambition is to cultivate a diverse, enduring professional network. By combining my existing technical background with advanced management education, I aim to connect with visionary individuals and position myself for impactful corporate leadership roles.
3. How do you handle working under pressure?
Navigating high pressure environments is a skill I honed within the fast paced startup ecosystem. Rather than panicking under tight deadlines, I maintain composure by pausing to breathe, breaking complex challenges into manageable components, and communicating proactively with stakeholders. I actually find that a moderate level of pressure enhances my efficiency and creative output.
4. How do you prioritize and manage your time effectively?
To manage my time effectively, I utilize a straightforward morning prioritization system. I tackle the most critical tasks early in the day when my energy peaks, strictly focusing on one assignment at a time to avoid the inefficiencies of multitasking. I also build buffer periods into my schedule for unexpected contingencies and conduct weekly reviews to ensure all deadlines are met.
5. Describe a challenging project you worked on and how you overcame obstacles.
This organized approach proved invaluable during a demanding corporate project where I led an analysis initiative alongside colleagues from non technical backgrounds. Facing a strict deadline, I evaluated the project requirements against my team capabilities. I opted to handle the most intricate coding responsibilities myself while delegating accessible data tasks to my peers. This strategic division of labor enabled us to deliver the final report on schedule.

6. How do you handle feedback and criticism?
I view criticism as a vital tool for professional growth. I do not take professional criticism personally, instead, I listen intently, ask clarifying questions, and diligently apply the feedback to improve my work. When a manager points out an error, I note the specific points and focus entirely on fixing the issue. I appreciate it when people point out my mistakes because it shows they want me to become better, and I treat it as a valuable lesson for my next tasks.
7. Tell me about a time when you had to make a difficult decision.
Committing to my MBA required making the most challenging decision of my career declining a hard earned Pre Placement Offer for my ideal technical role which is in AI space. After carefully weighing the immediate benefits of the job against the strategic advantages of an MBA, I chose to pursue the latter. I wrote down all the pros and cons and realized that while the job was good for now, the business acumen and network from an IIM are necessary for the next decade of my career.
8. Describe a situation where you demonstrated leadership skills.
My problem solving and leadership skills were tested during a collegiate hackathon. As team leader, I oversaw the development of an AI model requiring a five hour training period. We discovered late in the process that a teammate had inadvertently used the wrong dataset, resulting in a low preliminary score. When team morale plummeted, I intervened to prevent infighting. I motivated the group to stay up all night to retrain the algorithm correctly, ultimately securing excellent marks. This reinforced my belief that effective leaders take immediate, constructive responsibility during crises.
9. How do you handle failure or setbacks?
When dealing with failure, I objectively analyze the root cause to prevent recurrence rather than letting it stop me. I feel sad initially, but I quickly sit down and check what went wrong in the process. I try to find out the exact mistake and often seek external perspectives from mentors because they might see something I missed. I believe failing is just a part of learning when trying new things. The goal is to get back up fast and use the failure to become smarter for my next project.
10. How do you stay updated on industry trends and developments?
To continuously expand my knowledge and stay actively on industry developments, I maintain an active presence on LinkedIn and regularly read in few articles on platforms like Medium. Furthermore, I follow educational YouTube channels such as CodeReport, which excel at distilling complex technological shifts into easier understanding. By blending these resources with regular peer discussions, I ensure my industry awareness remains sharp and up to date.
